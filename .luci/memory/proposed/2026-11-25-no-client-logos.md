# Proposed rule: no client logos as standalone imagery

**Rule:** Case-study imagery never uses a client's logo as a standalone asset (logo stickers, logo walls, logo on a cover). Screenshots and screen recordings of a client's live site or app are fine, even when its header shows the logo. Luci's own logo and mascot are the exception (Lucas's product).

**Why:** Lucas, 2026-11-25: "Do not just use their logo except Luci which we built." The Classic Decoder logo was removed from the Classic Decoder App cover for this reason.

**Where it would go:** AGENTS.md / CLAUDE.md §4 as hard rule 19, and `.luci/rules.md`.
