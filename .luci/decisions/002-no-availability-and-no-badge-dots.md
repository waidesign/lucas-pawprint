# Decision · Remove availability badges & remove decorative dots in badges

- **Date:** 2026-11-24
- **Status:** Approved by Lucas

## Decisions
1. **No availability / remote-region text:** Do not show `"Open to remote roles · US · CA · NZ · SG"` or similar remote-availability stickers anywhere across the site (`Hero`, `ContactPanel`, `About`, `Contact`, `/lab`, etc.).
2. **No dots in badges:** Badges, skill pills, ticket stubs, `BetaBadge`, `Chip`, and `Eyebrow` must not render decorative leading dots.
