## 2. Brand idea

**Tagline:** *Curious by nature, careful by habit.*

**Hero line:** *Think outside the box with Lucas & Loaffy.*

**Smash line:** *Everyone designs with AI now. It all looks like slop. Except mine.*

**The idea in one sentence:** a calm, carefully built page with one lively character on it. The page is quiet and ordered; Loaffy brings the curiosity and the motion.

**Portfolio as a product:** Luci (Lucas's own product) leads the work, and the site itself is a case study too. Every detail (tokens, states, accessibility, motion) is deliberate and visible on the `/colophon` page.

## 3. Personality

| Trait | What it means | How it shows on the site | What it is NOT |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Curious** | Asks why, explores, notices | Loaffy's eyes follow the cursor; hover notes explain design decisions; small easter eggs | Nosy, random, cluttered |
| **Chill** | Calm, unhurried, friendly | Cloud background, lots of space, slow blink, plain words | Lazy, sloppy, vague |

| **Detail-obsessed** | Every pixel has a reason | 4px grid, tokens only, perfect states, the colophon page | Fussy copy, over-decoration |

## 4. Voice and tone

**Voice (always):** short sentences, plain words, first person, a wink but never a joke at the reader's expense.

| Scale | Position |
|---|---|
| Funny ↔ Serious | 40 / 60 (serious about work, light around it) |
| Casual ↔ Formal | 70 / 30 |
| Playful ↔ Matter-of-fact | 50 / 50 |
| Enthusiastic ↔ Calm | 30 / 70 |

**Rules**
- Write like you talk to a hiring manager over coffee.
- Lead with what happened, not adjectives. "I rebuilt the checkout" beats "I crafted a seamless, intuitive checkout experience".
- One idea per sentence. Aim for under 20 words.
- **Never invent numbers.** No percentages, conversion lifts or user counts unless Lucas has a real source. Describe outcomes qualitatively ("shipped", "replaced", "now used for…").
- No buzzwords: *seamless, cutting-edge, synergy, leverage, robust, world-class, pixel-perfect, delightful, elevate*.
- Loaffy can speak in short UI moments (toasts, 404, empty states). Lucas speaks everywhere else.
- Use US English spelling on the site (global audience): *color, optimize*.

## 5. Logo and wordmark

- **Wordmark:** `lucas` set in Bricolage Grotesque 800, tracking −0.02em, followed by a **ginger toe bean** in place of a full stop.
- The bean is an ellipse (rx 8, ry 11 in a 20×26 box), ginger `#F4A878`, 2.5px ink outline.
- **Minimum size:** 20px cap height (24px wordmark height). Clear space on all sides: the height of the bean.
- **Favicon / app icon:** Loaffy's calm face on a Lilac square (corner radius 8px at 32px, 40px at 180px). At 16px, use the simplified face (pad + 4 beans + eyes, no cheeks, no highlight).
- **Pawprint umbrella:** in the footer, "Part of Pawprint · pawprint.design" in caption style, next to a link to Luci.

## 6. Colour (summary; full tokens in DESIGN.md)

**Hard rules**
- Never `#000000`. Base black is **Ink `#2B2B2B`**.
- Never `#FFFFFF`. The lightest colour is **Cloud `#F6F6F3`**.
- Pastels only. No saturated, high-contrast accents.
- **Ginger is Loaffy's colour only.** Never use ginger for UI (buttons, links, focus rings, backgrounds). This keeps the site distinct from Luci (luci.pawprint.design), which uses ginger as its brand colour.

**Loaffy Candy palette**

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud | `#F6F6F3` | Page background (light) |
| Paper | `#ECECE6` | Surfaces, quiet sections |
| Ink | `#2B2B2B` | All text on light and pastel backgrounds, outlines, sticker shadows |
| Lilac | `#E2D3FF` | Section and card colour; text highlight on headlines |
| Mint | `#AEE8CC` | Section and card colour; success |
| Butter | `#FFE591` | Section and card colour; warning; "Except mine" highlight |
| Sky | `#AED6FF` | Section and card colour; info; primary-button offset shadow |
| Blush | `#FFC9D5` | Section and card colour; error (always with an icon and text) |
| Pistachio | `#D7EEA6` | Section and card colour; footer |
| Ginger | `#F4A878` | **Loaffy only** |

Ink on every pastel passes WCAG AAA (9.3:1 to 11.4:1).

**Section rhythm:** one pastel per section, and neighbouring sections never share a colour.

## 7. Typography (summary)

- **Display: Bricolage Grotesque** (600–800, optical size on). Big, tight, confident. Used for headlines, section words, stickers, the wordmark.
- **Body: Inter** (400, 500, 600). Quiet and readable. Used for paragraphs, UI, labels.
- Two type roles only (Dice.fm discipline). No third family except a mono for code snippets on the colophon page.
- Headline highlight: one word per headline may get a pastel highlight pill (Atoms.dev idea), never more than one.

---

## 8. Graphic language

- **Pen-drawn outlines.** Characters have a hand-drawn outline: thinner on the top-left, heavier on the bottom-right, with a slight wobble.
- **Stickers.** Cards, buttons and callouts use a 2px ink border and a **solid offset shadow** (no blur), like a sticker on paper.
- **Shapes come from the paw.** Toe bean → pill (buttons, chips). Main pad → 32px card corner. Four beans → loader, stepper, bullets.
- **No overlapping shapes inside a character.** Depth is shaded inside one outline.
- **No gradients, no glassmorphism, no glow, no emoji, no stock photos.**

---

## 9. Imagery

- Case study covers: real screens in simple device frames on a single pastel. Screens are the hero; frames stay thin and quiet.
- Loaffy may appear next to a cover (peeking, at about 10–20% of the cover's height), never on top of the screen content.
- Portrait of Lucas: warm, natural light, plain background, cropped in a 32px-radius frame.
- Alt text describes what matters in the image in one sentence: "Classic Decoder homepage on desktop and mobile, showing the VIN search."

---

## 10. Brand don'ts

- Don't use ginger outside Loaffy.
- Don't put more than one character on screen at a time (Loaffy or the cat hand, not both, except the cursor).
- Don't stretch, recolour or rotate Loaffy outside the documented poses.
- Don't use unverified numbers or invented testimonials.
- Don't use more than one highlighted word per headline.
- Don't use blurred shadows, gradients or pure black or white.

## 11. Luci inside the portfolio

- Luci keeps **its own brand** (ginger + Muzzle White `#F5EFEA`) inside its case study visuals and screenshots. The portfolio UI around it stays Loaffy Candy.
- Loaffy and Luci's cat are relatives, not twins: Loaffy is a pawprint with a face; Luci's mascot is Luci's own. Never merge them.
