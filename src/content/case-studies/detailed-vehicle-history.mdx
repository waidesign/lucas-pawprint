<Chapter step="understand" title="Taming a 20+ page automotive data platform">
  Detailed Vehicle History provides accident history, title checks, theft records, open recalls, and factory window stickers for cars, motorcycles, RVs, trucks, and classic vehicles.

  Over time, the legacy WordPress site had grown into a patchwork of mismatched landing pages. Mobile visitors struggled to switch between VIN decoding, license plate lookup, and window sticker tools, and the checkout flow lacked visual consistency.
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="shape" title="One scalable system across every vehicle category">
  Rather than styling 20+ pages one by one, I shaped a modular page architecture and component library that could power every lookup tool and CMS-driven manufacturer page.

<Chapter step="design" title="20+ core pages, design tokens, and checkout clarity">
  I led the UI/UX design across the platform's entire surface area:

  - **Core product pages:** Home Page, Vehicle History Report, Window Sticker Generator, VIN Decoder, License Plate Lookup, Title Check, Warranty Checker, Engine Type Decoder, Service Records, VIN Year Chart, and the Checkout Flow.
  - **Mobile-first inputs:** Single-field search bars with automatic VIN and license plate formatting for on-the-go lookups.
  - **Scalable Figma design system:** Standardized color contrast tokens, 8pt spacing, component states, and micro-interactions.
