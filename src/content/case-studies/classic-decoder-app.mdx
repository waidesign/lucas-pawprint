<Chapter step="understand" title="On-the-lot inspections on a phone">
  Classic car collectors and restorers often decode VINs on their phones while standing next to a car at an auction or swap meet.

  The earlier mobile app had fallen behind the redesigned website. Its decoding forms felt cramped on small screens, report credits and receipts were hard to track, and the Digital Garage did not sync cleanly with how owners actually organized their cars.
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="shape" title="Two lookup paths and one shared engine">
  Instead of maintaining two separate decoding engines across web and mobile, we shaped the mobile redesign around shared logic and clear branching for different classic VIN formats.

  <DecisionCard
    title="Two-path Yes/No VIN lookup flow"
    problem="Pre-1981 car makers used different VIN lengths and plate layouts, which confused users when forced into one rigid input."
    decision="Designed two guided decoding paths connected by a simple Yes/No prompt based on what the user has in front of them."
    why="Collectors can decode rare or non-standard VIN plates without guessing which format rules apply."
    tone="mint"
  />

  <DecisionCard
    title="Integrating the web decoding engine inside the mobile shell"
    problem="Rebuilding the entire pre-1981 decoding logic separately in native code risked long delays and mismatched results between web and mobile."
    decision="Embedded the responsive web decoding engine inside the native app shell with native navigation, account controls, and garage storage around it."
    why="Kept search behavior identical across platforms and let the engineering team ship faster."
    tone="sky"
  />
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="design" title="Digital Garage, account hub, and cross-platform tokens">
  I aligned the app's typography, color tokens, and component states with the Classic Decoder web system while tailoring navigation and touch targets for one-handed phone use.

  - **Refreshed Digital Garage:** Owners can store decoded classic cars, review full history reports and build sheets offline-ready on their phone, and re-download past documents.
  - **Credits & Help Center:** Added clear report credit tracking, purchase receipts, notification controls, and an in-app Help Center so buyers can resolve questions on their own.
  - **Mobile micro-interactions:** Added lightweight Lottie feedback for VIN scanning and garage updates.
