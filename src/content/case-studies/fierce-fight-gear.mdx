<Chapter step="understand" title="From manual email quotes to live customization">
  Fierce Fight Gear manufactures custom boxing gloves, Muay Thai shorts, MMA gear, and protective equipment in Thailand for fighters and gyms worldwide.

  Before the redesign, ordering custom gear meant emailing requests back and forth and waiting for manual proofs. Fighters could not see how metallic leather, piping colors, and custom embroidery looked together before placing an order, and shopping on a phone was cumbersome.
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="shape" title="A step-by-step customizer canvas that works on a phone">
  The customizer was the heart of the project. A single pair of gloves has dozens of customizable panels, materials, and logo zones, which can easily overwhelm a small mobile screen.

  <DecisionCard
    title="Guided canvas customizer with live price updates"
    problem="Exposing every leather panel, strap option, and embroidery field at once overwhelmed mobile shoppers."
    decision="Structured the customizer around a pinned live visual canvas with a step-by-step bottom control bar that updates the product render and cart total in real time."
    why="Fighters see every material and color change immediately on the glove or shorts without losing their place in the flow."
    tone="blush"
  />
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="design" title="Storefront, product pages, and fighter gallery">
  Alongside the customizer web app, I designed the complete e-commerce storefront:

  - **Real-time customizer controls:** Material swatches (matte, metallic, genuine leather), zone-by-zone color pickers, text/embroidery placement, and live price calculation.
  - **Product detail pages:** High-detail photography, sizing guides, and clear paths between ready-made stock and custom builds.
  - **Action gallery:** A real-world gallery of custom gear worn by fighters in the ring so new customers can see finished craftsmanship before designing their own.
