<Chapter step="understand" title="What was broken on the old site">
  Classic Decoder decodes VINs and provides historical build sheets for pre-1981 classic vehicles. Classic cars before 1981 use non-standard VIN formats that vary across manufacturers, which makes lookup harder than modern 17-character VINs.

  The legacy WordPress site asked visitors to fill out a long form before telling them if a record existed. Most visitors arrived on mobile while inspecting a car in person, and the old layout was hard to use on a phone.

  - **Upfront friction:** Visitors had to complete 14 form fields before seeing if their VIN matched a vehicle in the database.
  - **Trust gap:** Classic car collectors wanted proof that the database recognized their specific year, make, and model before paying.
  - **Missing mobile parity:** On-the-lot buyers needed a fast, readable lookup flow on small screens.
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="shape" title="Restructuring the lookup flow and marketplace">
  Moving from WordPress to React and Next.js gave us a clean slate. I restructured the product around two core journeys: instant VIN verification and a new classic-car classifieds marketplace.

  <DecisionCard
    title="3-step progressive VIN lookup instead of a 14-field form"
    problem="Asking for 14 fields upfront caused visitors to leave before running a search."
    decision="Reduced the hero entry to a single VIN input that pings the database immediately and reveals the remaining steps in a progressive drawer."
    why="Visitors get immediate confirmation that their classic VIN is supported before entering checkout details."
    tone="sky"
  />

  <DecisionCard
    title="Live decoded spec preview cards before payment"
    problem="Buyers hesitated to pay without knowing what data the report actually contained for their car."
    decision="Designed a live preview card showing the decoded year, make, model, and engine specs with locked report sections clearly outlined."
    why="Showing real decoded vehicle data upfront proved authenticity and removed guesswork at checkout."
    tone="mint"
  />
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="design" title="Design system, classifieds, and motion feedback">
  I built a new Figma design system from scratch for the Next.js rebuild, covering typography, color tokens, form states, landing pages, and inner tools.

  - **Online classifieds from scratch:** For buyers, I designed smart filters, listing cards, detail pages, and photo galleries. For sellers, I mapped the listing flow covering car specs, photo uploads, privacy settings, pricing, and VIN-linked report upsells.
  - **Buyer & seller dashboard:** Buyers can save cars and manage search alerts. Sellers can manage listings, message buyers, and track notifications.
  - **Motion feedback during database lookups:** Pre-1981 archive lookups take a moment to resolve, so I designed lightweight Lottie scanning animations in After Effects to explain what the system is checking.
