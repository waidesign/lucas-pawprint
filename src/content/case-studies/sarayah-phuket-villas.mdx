<Chapter step="understand" title="A first digital home for a Phuket villa retreat">
  Sarayah Phuket Villas is a private retreat of Two-Bedroom and Four-Bedroom Pool Villas near Surin, Yamu, Bang Tao, and Mai Khao beaches in Phuket.

  At launch, the estate needed its very first digital presence. International travelers wanted clear floor plans, privacy details, nearby beach distances, and transparent calendar availability without being bounced to a generic external booking page or waiting on manual email inquiries.
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="shape" title="Direct Hostex booking and an AI-first rebuild">
  I led the initial UI/UX design and later returned to rebuild the entire site using an AI-first workflow, combining Figma, Google AI Studio, and Google Antigravity to go from design direction to a live production web app.

<Chapter step="design" title="Villa showcases, local guide, and calm editorial layout">
