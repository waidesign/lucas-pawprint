<Chapter step="understand" title="Why API buyers got stuck on the old pricing tables">
  Vehicle Databases offers more than 25 automotive data APIs, including VIN decoding, license plate OCR, market value, maintenance, repair, and title checks.

  Inside the member dashboard, engineering leads and fleet operators had to compare monthly subscriptions, annual commitments, and pay-as-you-go credits across static tables. Many could not tell which tier fit their call volume without emailing sales first.
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="shape" title="Interactive quota slider and custom quote builder">
  I reshaped the pricing page so self-serve developers can calculate their exact tier in seconds, while enterprise buyers with custom endpoint mixes can request a tailored quote without leaving the dashboard.

  <DecisionCard
    title="Interactive API-call slider that recommends a plan"
    problem="Static tier tables forced buyers to do mental math on cost-per-call and overage thresholds."
    decision="Designed an interactive slider where users drag their expected monthly API calls and immediately see the recommended package, unit price, and included endpoints."
    why="Replaced guesswork with an instant recommendation and let buyers move straight to checkout with confidence."
    tone="butter"
  />

  <DecisionCard
    title="Transparent checkout order summary with annual switch card"
    problem="Buyers reaching checkout were unsure which APIs were bundled and missed the savings of annual billing."
    decision="Built a clear order summary listing every selected API alongside a one-click card to switch from monthly to yearly billing."
    why="Kept the checkout summary unambiguous and made billing options visible right when the buyer reviews their total."
    tone="mint"
  />
</Chapter>

<Chapter step="design" title="Dashboard pricing, comparison matrix, and enterprise quotes">
  I designed the full pricing and checkout flow inside the existing user dashboard:

  - **Plan & feature comparison:** Clear monthly, yearly, and pay-as-you-go views with sticky mobile columns so developers can compare endpoints on any screen.
  - **Custom quote builder:** Buyers can select specific APIs, describe custom dataset requirements not listed in standard tiers, or book a meeting with sales directly from the pricing view.
